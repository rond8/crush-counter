Publishing an app on the Google Play Console involves a structured series of prerequisite steps. Completing them in order prevents submission delays and review rejections.

Google Play Publishing Checklist

1.Set Up & Verify Your Developer Account:Day 1.

Register: Pay the $25 one-time fee and choose between a Personal or Organization account.

Verify Identity: Upload a government ID (and D-U-N-S number if registering as an organization). Note: Verification can take up to a few business days.

Enable 2-Step Verification: Required for all Play Console accounts.

2.Create Your App & Set Up the Main Store Listing:Day 2.

Go to All apps > Create app in the Play Console.

App Details: Enter App Name (max 30 chars), Short Description (max 80 chars), and Full Description (max 4,000 chars).

Graphics Upload:

App Icon: 512 × 512 px PNG (full square, no rounded corners).

Feature Graphic: 1024 × 500 px PNG/JPEG.

Screenshots: Minimum 2 (up to 😎 for phone, 16:9 or 9:16 aspect ratio.

Categorization & Support: Select your app category and provide a active public support email address.

3.Complete App Content Declarations:Day 2 - 3.

Navigate to Policy & Programs > App content and complete all required questionnaires:

Privacy Policy: Provide an active HTTPS URL to your hosted privacy policy.

Data Safety: Detail what user data your app collects, shares, or encrypts (including third-party SDKs like Firebase or AdMob).

Content Rating: Answer the IARC questionnaire to obtain official regional age ratings.

Target Audience: Specify your age target groups and fill out children's policy declarations if applicable.

App Access Credentials: If your app requires login, provide demo/test account credentials for Google's review team.

Ads & Financial Declarations: Declare whether your app contains ads or financial/health features.

4.Build Your Android App Bundle (.aab):Day 4.

Format: Build an Android App Bundle (.aab)—standalone APKs are no longer accepted for new apps.

Target SDK: Ensure your project targets the latest Android API level requirement (Android 15 / API 35 or higher).

App Signing: Enroll in Google Play App Signing. Keep your local upload keystore file safe.

5.Run Closed Testing (Personal Accounts Only):Min. 14 Days.

If you are using a personal account, Google requires a closed test before production publishing.

Recruit at least 12-20 opted-in testers who keep the app installed for 14 consecutive days.

Fix bugs and push update bundles to the testing track during this period if needed.

6.Submit for Production Review:Final Step.

Go to Testing > Production (or request production access after testing).

Create a new release, upload your finalized .aab, and enter Release Notes.

Select your target countries/regions.

Review your summary for any warnings and click Start rollout to Production.

Review Time: Typically takes 3–7 days for first-time app submissions.
